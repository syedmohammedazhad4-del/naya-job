# NayaJob — Problem Statement

> The govt + money checklist no one told you about when you started your first job.

---

## TL;DR

Every year, ~1.5 million Indians in their early 20s take their first formal job. They sign 14 pages of HR docs they don't understand, pick the wrong tax regime, fail to activate their UAN, skip independent health insurance, miss ITR deadlines, and pay an invisible "fresher tax" of **₹15,000–₹50,000 in their first year alone** — entirely from preventable confusion.

NayaJob fixes this. It's a personalized 12-month roadmap for the first year of working life in India. Four-question intake → 30–40 ranked action items → step-by-step guides → reminders → mark-as-done tracking. Free roadmap. ₹399 one-time unlocks WhatsApp reminders + AI assistant.

## 1. The Problem

### Who is affected

A specific, real person:

> *Ravi, 22, from Warangal. Graduated from JNTU. Got placed at TCS in May 2026. Moving to Hyderabad. CTC ₹4.5 LPA. He has never:*
> - *Filed a tax return*
> - *Heard of Form 12BB or UAN*
> - *Read his offer letter's salary breakup*
> - *Bought independent health insurance*
> - *Linked PAN with Aadhaar*
> - *Made a rent agreement*

Ravi is not unusual. **He is the median fresh hire in Indian IT.** Multiply him by 1.5 million.

### What goes wrong (real costs)

In his first 12 months, Ravi will, on average:

| Mistake | Cost |
|---|---|
| Pick the New tax regime because it sounds modern, when Old would have saved him ₹15,000 with HRA + 80C | ₹15,000 / year |
| Forget to submit Form 12BB on time → excess TDS for 6 months → refund stuck till next year | Cash flow stress |
| Not activate UAN → PF stuck for entire career, can't transfer on job switch | Compounded loss |
| Skip independent health insurance because company gave one → switches jobs → mother needs surgery in the gap | Catastrophic |
| Miss the 31 July ITR deadline | ₹1,000–5,000 fine + interest |
| Not e-verify ITR within 30 days → filing invalid → notice in October | Stress + notice |
| Pay credit card minimum due → 40% interest builds quietly | ₹10,000+ / year |
| Not collect rent receipts → can't claim HRA → ₹30,000–₹60,000 lost | ₹30,000+ / year |
| Forget to update Aadhaar address → KYC fails on bank, mobile, scheme verification | Operational chaos |

Total invisible cost of fresher confusion in year 1: **₹20,000–₹80,000.**
Compounded over a career: **lakhs.**

### Why this problem is acute right now

1. **Volume.** India added 1.5M+ fresh hires per year in formal employment (NASSCOM, EPFO data). The largest entry cohort to the financial system, ever.
2. **Frequency.** Every fresher faces these exact 40 things, in the same order, in the same 12 months.
3. **Time-sensitivity.** Tax regime, Form 12BB, ITR — all deadline-driven. Miss = pay.
4. **Emotional load.** Fresh hires feel guilty for not knowing. Parents (often govt/PSU background) don't know IT-job specifics. Friends shrug.
5. **No existing trusted source.** Information is fragmented across 200+ govt and finance sites.

## 2. Why This Problem Exists

This is a market failure built on five compounding factors:

### a. Government systems are functional but UX-hostile
EPFO, IT Dept, UIDAI portals work — but are designed for compliance officers, not for a confused 22-year-old. Jargon, dropdown menus four levels deep, error messages in officialese.

### b. HR onboarding is paperwork, not pedagogy
The "joining kit" is a 14-page PDF designed to protect the company legally, not to educate the employee. Nobody reads it. Nobody is meant to.

### c. Indian families' knowledge doesn't transfer
The previous generation worked in PSUs, govt, or family businesses. They know agriculture income tax, FD interest, gold loans. They don't know HRA exemption rules for a software engineer renting in Madhapur.

### d. Education ignores this entirely
4 years of engineering at JNTU teaches no tax, no insurance, no investing, no docs. The first salary lands and the person is alone with the system.

### e. The internet has failed this demographic specifically
- **Govt sites**: technically correct, UX-broken
- **Finance influencers**: entertainment-grade, not action-grade
- **SEO content farms**: low quality, often outdated, conflicting
- **CA blogs**: behind paywalls or for already-knowledgeable readers
- **General AI chatbots** (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity): hallucinate on Indian tax/govt rules — the worst possible domain to be wrong in

The result: every fresher solves the same 40 problems alone, painfully, and with avoidable mistakes.

| Solution | What it does | Why it doesn't solve this |

| **HR onboarding docs** | Legal/compliance paperwork | Designed for HR's protection, not the employee's understanding. |
| **YouTube finance influencers** | General financial education | Entertainment-grade. Doesn't translate to "do *this* by *this* date for *your* situation." |
| **General AI chatbots** | Conversational interface | Hallucinate confidently on Indian tax/govt rules. Cannot be trusted in this domain. |
| **CAs / financially-aware friends** | Personalized advice | Slow, gatekept, expensive (₹3,000–₹15,000 for a CA consult). Not scalable. |

**The gap:** there is no product that *coordinates* the action items, *personalizes* them to the individual, *grounds* the answers in verified sources, and *follows through* with reminders.

That's the product.

## 4. What NayaJob Does

A **personalized 12-month roadmap** for the first year of working life in India.

### The user experience, end to end

1. **Lands on the site.** One sentence: *"The govt + money checklist no one told you about when you started your first job."*
2. **60-second intake.** Four questions: joining month, CTC band, city, living situation.
3. **Personalized roadmap generated.** 30–40 action items, filtered by their profile, sorted by urgency:
   - **🔴 Do this week** (PAN-Aadhaar link, UAN activation, Form 12BB, tax regime choice, nominee setup)
   - **🟡 Do this month** (health insurance, rent agreement, salary account, emergency fund start)
   - **🟢 Do this year** (ITR filing, 80C top-up, NPS, Form 16 review)
   - **⚪ Situational** (job switch PF transfer, lost PAN replacement, DL state transfer)
4. **Each item is a complete guide.**
   - Why it matters (1–2 plain-English sentences)
   - 3–5 step how-to
   - Documents needed
   - Deadline
   - Link to the official government source
   - "Verified as of [date]" stamp
   - Mark-as-done toggle
5. **Reminders.** Email (free) or WhatsApp (premium) before each deadline.
6. **AI assistant.** Asks questions, answers strictly from NayaJob's verified content (RAG-grounded — no hallucination).
7. **Progress visible.** A bar updates as items get marked done. Sense of momentum.

### Pricing
- **Free**: roadmap + all guides + email reminders + 5 AI questions/month
- **Premium ₹399 one-time, lifetime for year one**: WhatsApp reminders, unlimited AI, downloadable templates (rent agreement, HRA declaration, resignation letter, ITR walkthrough video), priority support, 30-day money-back guarantee
- **B2B (future)**: ₹50–₹100 per fresher onboarded for IT companies' HR teams to bundle as an onboarding benefit

## 5. How It Works (Under the Hood)

| Layer | Implementation |
|---|---|
| **Content** | 40 hand-curated items, each with title, why, steps, docs, deadline, source, verified-as-of date. Source of truth lives in `src/data/items.ts` — versioned in git, reviewed monthly. |
| **Personalization** | Rule-based engine. Each item has an `appliesIf(profile)` function. Profile filters + urgency-based sort happen client-side. |
| **State** | localStorage (v0.2). Supabase Postgres + Row Level Security (v0.5+). |
| **Search / Chat** | Keyword search over items (v0.2). Gemini 2.0 Flash + Supabase pgvector RAG over the same content (v0.5+). |
| **Reminders** | Resend email API (free tier). AiSensy / Gupshup WhatsApp Business API (~₹2,500/mo). |
| **Payments** | Razorpay one-time + subscription. |
| **Hosting** | GitHub Pages for static (v0.2). Vercel for full-stack (v0.5+). |
| **Stack** | Next.js 14 App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind, Fraunces + Inter fonts. |

### Why this stack
- **No backend in v0.2** → ₹0/month operational cost while we validate
- **Content in git** → reviewable, dated, versioned, no CMS overhead
- **Static export** → 50ms page loads, infinite scale
- **Gemini Flash + RAG (later)** → near-free AI with strict factual grounding

## 9. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Tax / govt rules change** | Monthly content review ritual. "Verified as of" stamp on every page. Major changes trigger same-day update + reminder blast. Public changelog. |
| **Competitor (e.g., ClearTax) expands into fresher onboarding** | Speed of execution. Brand of "made for first-jobbers" specifically. Multi-language. Open data attracts community. |
| **Govt launches a competing portal** | Unlikely to do UX well. We compete on craft, not on data. |
| **Liability for wrong info** | Explicit disclaimer ("informational, not legal/tax advice"). Every claim cited. 24-hour fix policy. |
| **Founder boredom (40 items isn't a lifetime of content)** | Planned Phase 2 product (Settled Earner) prevents stagnation. B2B partnerships keep founder engaged with customer-facing work. |
| **Distribution monoculture** | Diversify across LinkedIn, Reddit, SEO, college placement partnerships, B2B HR deals. |
| **AI hallucination** | RAG with strict system prompt: "Answer ONLY from provided context. If not in context, say 'I don't know' + cite official source." Test suite of trick questions. |
| **Trust collapse from one wrong answer** | Open data, fast-fix culture, user-reported errors fixed within 24h, transparent versioning. |
